# ♠ Guandan Counter ♥

**Your scorekeeper for game night.** Track both teams' levels from 2 all the way to A, fix any mistake with one tap, and find out once and for all who the best partnership at the table is.

**Open it here: https://fei7fei.github.io/guandan-counter/**

Shuffle up and have fun! 🃏

---

## What it does

- **Two ways to play.** After the opening animation, pick **掼蛋 Guandan** (climb from 2 to A) or **转蛋 Zhuandan** (always on 2, play for money), and choose **Quick game** (nothing saved) or **Track stats** right there. Switch any time with the mode button at the top of the Play screen.
- **Big playing-card scoreboard.** Each team's level shows as a card you can read from across the table. It flips over every time a team climbs.
- **Super simple scoring.** Tap the team that won, then tap **+1**, **+2** or **+3**. That's it! The app handles the rules on A for you.
- **Knows the rules.** Shows whose level is being played and which heart is wild, and handles the "you must win on A" rule. By default a team gets 3 tries at A before dropping back to 2, and you can change house rules like "1-4 counts on A" or "unlimited tries on A".
- **Mistakes are no big deal.** Undo the last hand, tap any earlier hand to edit or delete it, and every level after it fixes itself.
- **Quick game or tracked game.** A quick game is the fastest way to start: type four names, or leave them as 1 2 3 4, and play. Nothing is saved. Switch to **Track stats** when you want the game to count.
- **Player stats.** Add your regulars once. The app tracks win rates for every player, every partnership and every head-to-head matchup. You can rename or delete players any time.
- **History.** Every tracked game is saved by date. You can resume an unfinished game whenever you like, or delete one you don't want.
- **Share with friends.** Send a game result or the leaderboard to Messages, WeChat, WhatsApp or anywhere else.
- **Spring! 春天！** Win the game without the other team taking a single hand and you get a special celebration, complete with falling blossoms and a voice that calls it out.
- **English and 中文.** Tap the 中文 / EN button to switch languages. It starts in Chinese on phones set to Chinese.
- **Fun to use.** Card flips, poker-chip buttons, sound effects and a confetti shower when a team wins. Sound can be turned off with the ♪ button.
- **A tune to open with.** As the chick waddles in, a little melody plays along: a note for every step, a boing for the hop, chimes as the aces fan out and sparkles as the suits light up. iPhones and iPads don't let web apps play sound before the first tap, so there the opening usually plays without music.
- **Works offline.** Once it's on your Home Screen, it works with no internet connection.
- **Turn it sideways for the big table.** Turn your phone and the game fills the whole screen: two giant level cards in 掼蛋, or everyone's money in big numbers in 转蛋, easy to read from anywhere around the table. Turn it back upright to record a hand, undo or settle up.

## Put it on your iPhone or iPad

There's no App Store and no install fee.

1. Open **https://fei7fei.github.io/guandan-counter/** in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open it from the new chick icon. It runs full screen, just like a regular app. 🎉

> **Tip:** iPhones and iPads mute web sounds when silent mode is on (the side switch or Control Center). Turn silent mode off to hear the card flips and the Spring call.

### Your data

Everything is saved **on the device** you're using. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. To move to a new device, or just to keep a backup:

- **Players tab → Export backup** saves a `.json` file (on iPhone or iPad you can choose *Save to Files*).
- **Players tab → Import backup** on the other device loads that file.

Export a backup every so often. It's cheap insurance for years of bragging rights.

## How to play Guandan (掼蛋)

These are the common rules. Plenty of tables have their own house rules, and the app lets you set the ones that change scoring.

### Setup

- **Four players in two teams.** Partners sit opposite each other, so play alternates between the teams.
- **Two full decks with all four jokers**, 108 cards in all. Everyone gets **27 cards**.

### Levels (级) and the wild card (逢人配)

- Each team has a **level**. Both teams start at **2** and climb toward **A**.
- Every hand is played at the level of the team that won the last hand. In the first hand, both teams are at 2.
- **The level card is promoted.** If you're playing 7s, every 7 ranks above the A.
- **The heart of the level rank is wild (逢人配).** When playing 7s, the two 7♥ can stand in for any card except a joker. They're the most useful cards in the deck.

**Card order, high to low:** red joker › black joker › level card › A › K › Q › J › 10 › 9 › 8 › 7 › 6 › 5 › 4 › 3 › 2

Suits don't matter for ranking. They only count for straight flushes.

### What you can play

| Play | Chinese | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single | 单张 | 8 |
| Pair | 对子 | 8 8 |
| Three of a kind | 三张 | 8 8 8 |
| Full house | 三带二 | 8 8 8 + 4 4 |
| Straight (exactly 5 cards) | 顺子 | 4 5 6 7 8 |
| Three pairs in a row | 三连对 (木板) | 4 4 5 5 6 6 |
| Two triples in a row | 三同连张 (钢板) | 4 4 4 5 5 5 |

- An A can go at either end of a run: A-2-3-4-5 is the lowest straight, 10-J-Q-K-A the highest. For straights and runs, the level card goes back to its normal place.
- To beat a play, put down **the same kind of play with the same number of cards, but higher**. A full house is compared by its three of a kind.

### Bombs (炸弹)

A bomb beats any ordinary play, whatever it is. Bombs beat each other in this order, from weakest to strongest:

1. **4 of a kind**
2. **5 of a kind**
3. **Straight flush** (同花顺): 5 cards in a row, all the same suit
4. **6 of a kind**, then **7**, then **8**
5. **All four jokers** (天王炸), which nothing beats

Between two bombs of the same size, the higher rank wins.

### Playing a hand

- The leader plays any valid play. Going around the table, each player either beats the last play or passes.
- When everyone else passes, whoever played last wins the trick and leads anything they like.
- **Passing the lead to your partner (接风).** If you play your last card and nobody beats it, your partner gets the lead.
- The hand goes on until three players are out. The finishing order is first out (头游), second (二游), third (三游) and last (末游).

### Who goes up

The team of the first-out player wins the hand. How far they climb depends on where the partner finished:

| Finish | Name | Levels gained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st and 2nd | 双下 (opponents are both last) | **+3** |
| 1st and 3rd | | **+2** |
| 1st and 4th | | **+1** |

The losing team stays where it is.

### Tribute (进贡) before each new hand

From the second hand onward, the losers pay tribute before play starts:

- **Single tribute (单贡):** the last-out player gives their **highest card** to the first-out player. The wild heart can't be given.
- **Double tribute (双贡):** after a 1-2 finish, both losers give their highest card. The higher card goes to first out, the other to second out.
- **Returning a card (还贡):** each player who received a card gives one back. It's usually any card **10 or lower**.
- **Refusing tribute (抗贡):** if the side that owes tribute holds **both red jokers**, no tribute is paid.
- **Who leads:** whoever paid tribute (in a double tribute, the one who gave the higher card). If tribute was refused, first out from the last hand leads.

Tribute doesn't change anyone's level, so you don't record it in the app.

### Winning the game: getting past A

- Reaching A isn't enough. Your team has to **win a hand while playing at A**.
- Under the usual rule that hand must be a **1-2 or 1-3 finish**. Winning 1-4 doesn't count and you stay on A. Some tables accept any win: choose **Any win counts** in the app's house rules.
- By default the app gives a team **3 tries at A**. Fail all three and the team drops **back to 2**. Tables that allow endless tries can pick **Missed tries on A → Unlimited**.
- Win at A and your team takes the whole game. Win it without the other team taking a single hand, and that's **Spring (春天)!**

## Zhuandan (转蛋): always on 2, play for money

Zhuandan uses all the same cards and plays as Guandan, with a few big differences:

- **Every hand is played on 2**, so **♥2 is always wild**. Nobody climbs levels.
- **There's no tribute** between hands.
- **Partners can change every hand.** Many tables pair up whoever is dealt the two ♠3s.
- **Money changes hands after every hand.** You agree on a base amount. The winning pair each collect it, and the losing pair each pay it:

| Winning team finishes | Each winner collects |
| --- | --- |
| 1st and 2nd | 3 × base |
| 1st and 3rd | 2 × base |
| 1st and 4th | 1 × base |

- **Bombs double it.** Each bomb your table counts doubles the payout, and **all four jokers** multiply it by 4. Tables differ on which bombs count (often 6+ card bombs and straight flushes) and whether both teams' bombs count, so agree before you start.

### Keeping score in the app

1. Pick **Zhuandan**, then choose the **base amount** and the money: **¥ RMB**, **$ CAD** or plain **points**.
2. **Count bombs as they land.** Tap **💣 Bomb!** every time someone bombs during the hand (and **4 jokers ×4** if they show up), so nobody has to remember at the end. The counter shows what the hand now pays.
3. After each hand, tap the team that won. Your bombs are already filled in. Fix the bomb count with **−** / **+** if needed, then tap **×1**, **×2** or **×3**. Each button shows how much every winner collects.
4. Everyone's running total sits on the table. Tap **Change partners** whenever the pairs switch, or tap a player to change their name mid-game.
5. Tap **Settle up** to see the totals and **who pays whom**, using the fewest payments possible. **Finish and save** puts the game in History.

Zhuandan games get their own **money leaderboard** in Stats (switch between 掼蛋 and 转蛋 at the top), with each player's net winnings, hands won, and how every partnership does together. Each player's page shows their Zhuandan record too.

Now go deal some cards. Good luck, and may your bombs always land! 💣♠♥♣♦
